I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at IESEG School of Management in Lille, France.

I hold a Ph.D. in Finance from HEC Montréal, where my thesis, *The Importance of Empirical Choices: Three Essays on Retail Trading and Hedging Strategies*, was supervised by Profs. David Ardia and Tolga Cenesizoglu.

My research covers asset pricing, retail investors, and portfolio management. I work primarily with econometric and machine learning methods applied to large-scale, high-frequency financial data, with a current focus on multi-design analyses.
